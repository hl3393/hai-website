About

About the Institute

A small nonprofit with one large question.

Our mission

Human Alignment Institute gathers stories of how people across every field live and work with AI. We ask not how much more efficient AI makes us, but how we remain human alongside it.

Our guiding idea

We value value rationality over instrumental rationality. Instrumental rationality asks how to reach a goal most efficiently; value rationality asks whether the goal is worth reaching, and what it does to us along the way. We don't reject tools. We ask where they lead us as people.

Questions we ask our contributors

When we invite someone to share their experience, we offer these questions as a starting point, not a form to fill in.

  1. How has AI changed the way you work or live?
  2. Has it also changed what you value?
  3. Is there anything you refuse to hand over to AI? Why?
  4. Where do you hope this is leading you as a person?

Haoyu Liu · Founder

I am a PhD student in Educational Policy and Leadership at the University at Albany, SUNY. I hold a master's degree from [Teachers College, Columbia University] and a bachelor's degree in education from the University of Oregon.

I was born in Gansu, China, and went to school in Beijing before coming to the United States, so I have spent my life moving between very different worlds. Two questions run through all of my work: why failures are so often blamed on individuals when their causes lie in structures, and whether education is shaping people into human beings or into something else. My current research looks at AI literacy policy and the ecological costs that technology makes invisible.

I founded Human Alignment Institute to gather the voices that conversations about AI usually leave out, and to ask, together with them, how we remain human alongside AI.
