How do we remain human alongside AI?

Human Alignment Institute gathers stories of how people across every field live and work with AI. We ask not how much more efficient AI makes us, but how we remain human alongside it.

Read the voices About us

Our guiding idea

Value rationality over instrumental rationality

Most conversations about AI ask an instrumental question: how can this tool get us to our goals faster? We ask a question of value: which goals are worth having, and who do we become as we pursue them?

We don't reject tools. We ask where they lead us as people. That is why we listen to teachers, artists, researchers, engineers and neighbours describe, in their own words, what living with AI is really like.
