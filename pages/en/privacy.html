Privacy

Your privacy

We built this site to be read, not to watch its readers. Here is everything it does.

In short

  • No cookies, no advertising, no tracking across websites.
  • We never ask for your location.
  • Nothing you do on this site is sold or shared.

Time of day

The site’s colours follow the sun, and the bar at the top shows today’s date, solar term, moon phase, sunrise and sunset. All of this is calculated in your own browser from your device’s clock and time zone. Nothing about it is sent to us or to anyone else.

What your browser remembers

If you choose Light or Dark under Appearance, your browser remembers that choice on your device so the next page looks the same. Choosing Auto removes it. You can also clear it at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

Videos

Some pieces include a video from YouTube, Vimeo or Bilibili. The player loads only on that piece’s page. We use YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode and Vimeo’s “do not track” setting, but once you play a video, that service’s own privacy policy applies.

Hosting

The site is served by Cloudflare. Like any web server, it briefly handles basic technical information, such as your IP address, in order to deliver pages to you and keep the site secure.

Contributors

We publish a contributor’s name, short biography, photo and pieces only with their permission. If you are a contributor and would like anything changed or removed, write to us and we will do it.
